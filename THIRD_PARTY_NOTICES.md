## Arc UI

The components in `apps/desktop/src/components/arc/` (toast stack, usage meter, donut chart,
progress, timeline, tree view, code block, copy button, tabs, drawer, rich text
editor, file dropzone, badge, text reveal, and their motion tokens) are adapted from the free
components of Arc UI — <https://uiarc.dev>, <https://github.com/kuratlielia/arc-library>.
Rafiq's changes: its own icons (Tabler), its theme tokens, right-to-left layout, and labels in
Arabic, English and Russian.

## EasyUI

button, airport matrix clock, skeleton, not-found and rainbow button) are adapted from EasyUI —
<https://easyui.site>, <https://github.com/Surajmaurya1/easyui>. Rafiq's changes: its theme
tokens, right-to-left layout, labels in Arabic, English and Russian, Arabic city names beside
the clock, an error screen built on the not-found page, and options with icons in the menu.
